Roughly half of Pennsylvania households can't put solar panels on their own homes. They rent, live in apartments, have a shaded roof, or can't cover the upfront cost. Community solar is built for exactly those households. It lets people subscribe to a share of a local solar project and receive credits on their electric bill, with nothing installed at home.

Our neighbors in New Jersey, New York, and Maryland already have community solar programs. Pennsylvania doesn't yet. A bill to create one, **HB 504**, passed the State House in May 2025 and is now waiting on the State Senate.
